You are built to blossom into more

Most people aren't built for quick pivots—and that's normal. That's why coaching exists. The ability to navigate change can be learned and developed.

My approach integrates insights from leading researchers—Angela Duckworth's work on grit, James Clear on habits, Brené Brown on resilience, Stephen Covey's principles, and the latest neuroplasticity science—into a practical framework that meets you exactly where you are.

The Kintsugi Philosophy

The Japanese art of Kintsugi repairs broken pottery with gold, making the cracks visible and beautiful rather than hiding them. Someone who has been broken and healed can become more beautiful and more lovable than ever before.

We are each other's wounded healers. Your story—your transitions, your struggles, your growth—is your credential. The gold is already in you.

Who I Help

She's not broken. She's just stuck.

Maybe she's 25 and trying to figure out who she is apart from everyone else's story for her. Maybe she's 45 and the kids are gone, the career feels empty, and there's a question she's been afraid to say out loud: is this it? Maybe she's 55 and just walked away from something she gave everything to — and has no idea what's supposed to come next.

Age doesn't define her. Her moment does.

She's smart. She's capable. She's probably the one everyone else calls when they need advice. But right now she can't find her own footing — and she's tired of pretending she's fine.

She doesn't need a cheerleader. She doesn't need someone to tell her she's amazing and it'll all work out. She needs someone who will look her situation straight in the eye, say the thing nobody else will say, and still be standing there when it's hard.

That's the work I do.

If any of this is you, let's talk:

You don't have to have it figured out to reach out. You just have to be ready to stop pretending you're fine.

One thing worth knowing: Bloom Reimagined is coaching, not therapy. I work with women who are ready to move forward and need someone honest in their corner. If what you're carrying requires clinical support, I'll tell you that too — and point you toward someone who can actually help.

Meet Your Coach

I spent 17 years as founding principal — building something from the ground up, pouring everything into it, and then walking away from it to figure out what came next. I know what it feels like to stand at the edge of a chapter you've closed and have no idea what the next one looks like. That's not a credential I got from a book. That's my life.

I've spent my career coaching people through change — students, parents, teachers, families. I've sat across from people in the hardest moments of their lives and said the thing nobody else would say. I've watched people discover they were capable of far more than they believed. That's the work I was made for.

The BLOOM Framework is built on research that actually works: Angela Duckworth's Grit, James Clear's Atomic Habits, Brené Brown's Rising Strong, Stephen Covey's 7 Habits, neuroplasticity science, and more. With a Master's degree in Leadership, I've synthesized what the best minds have learned about transformation into something practical — because theory without application is just interesting reading.

I'm not here to cheerlead you or hand you a workbook and call it coaching. I'm here to tell you the truth and stand with you while you prove to yourself that you can handle it.
